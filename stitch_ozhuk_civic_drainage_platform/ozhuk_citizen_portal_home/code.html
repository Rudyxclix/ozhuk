Citizen Quick Guide

Reporting Takes Under 60 Seconds

Your direct reporting prevents urban waterlogging, preserves roadway integrity, and protects downstream water bodies.

Step 01

Snap Clean Evidence

Take one wide angle to show street context and one close-up detailing the debris blockage. Natural light ensures fast automated AI triage.

lightbulb Tip: Avoid zooming. Stand 2 paces back to record street landmarks.
Step 02

Confirm Location Pin

Our GIS instantly suggests the correct ward jurisdiction. If you are reporting from home after seeing the clog, easily drag the pin on the map.

security Zero personal tracking. Only the physical asset coordinates are recorded.
Step 03

Track Resolution Live

Receive automated SMS or browser notifications when the municipal crew arrives and once hydraulic clearance verification is confirmed.

verified Every ticket is backed by public municipal accountability records.
